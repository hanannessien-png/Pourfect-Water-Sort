Privacy Policy

Pourfect: Water Sort Puzzle

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Apex Interactive (“we”) provides Pourfect: Water Sort Puzzle. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Game data

No account is required. Your progress, coins, unlocked items and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your game progress, and the game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Advertising and third parties

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device and advertising identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. These providers process information under their own policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Your choices

Where required, a consent message lets you choose how your information is used for advertising. You can revisit these choices through “Privacy settings” in the game when available. You can also manage advertising privacy controls in your Android settings. Internet access is needed for ads, but not for the core puzzle gameplay.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it, subject to any device backup settings. Clearing storage deletes your saved progress. Google and its partners determine retention for data they process; use their privacy controls or contact them for deletion requests. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in their country. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be shown on this page.
